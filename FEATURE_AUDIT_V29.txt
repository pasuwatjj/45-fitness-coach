45 Fitness Coach v29 — รายการตรวจความครบถ้วน / Feature audit
24 October 2026 — complete source package; not deployed

This release contains all previous modules, not only theme changes. The actual
behavior of protected features depends on the signed-in account, VIP/admin role
and configured services. A guest seeing a sign-in message is not a missing page.

13 page entry points present in the delivered template
01 Home: original 45 logo, full overview, muscle groups, exercise images and VIP locks.
   New original Spicylifter bunny/cloud or Powerlifter ghost/pumpkin hero + icons.
02 Workout: 15 exercises, images/videos, camera preview, fullscreen, start/pause/
   resume/stop/reset/calibrate, saved sets, tutorial, angle/tolerance controls,
   Beginner/Pro/Expert, automatic arm switching and compact voice settings.
03 Calendar: month/day plan editor, per-set credits, daily completion and streak.
04 Leaderboard: saved training ranking, side/mode filters and streaks.
05 Cardio: jumping jacks/pushups, upper/full body mode, timer, rewards and rankings.
06 Health: private measurements, BMI summary, saved history and progress.
07 Character: account-owned appearance, muscle growth, keyboard/pointer rotation,
   animated dumbbell curl, read-only member preview and saved style after login.
08 Profile: history, charts, muscle totals, photos, privacy/share controls,
   character animation in own and shared member profiles.
09 VIP: status/expiry, real local payment QR, receipt file form, visible failure/retry,
   server-side premium gates; configured verification or manual review.
10 Rewards: four training currencies, exchange buttons for signed-in accounts,
   VIP whey redemption, order/history, stable retries, legacy balance separation.
11 Challenges: progress and daily completed-plan reward claims.
12 Friends: search, incoming requests, accept/cancel, member profiles and private chat.
13 Admin: role-protected member list/search/paging, profiles, VIP grant, receipt
   review, maintenance and site controls. Hidden from normal accounts as before.

Retained v28 repairs (included even if v28 was never installed)
- Navigation tolerates an absent unrelated character element: rewards/VIP still load.
- Clear incomplete-upload notice, rewards/VIP visible errors and retry actions.
- Per-email character persistence; guest and other-account isolation.
- Own/member profile curl animation, RAF cleanup, <=30fps, offscreen and reduced motion.
- v26 navigation dimensions; intentionally removed refresh/decorative dumbbell,
  three side observation cards and outside-camera cue/distance/sound controls
  remain hidden. Their underlying live IDs remain for processing compatibility.
- Small voice settings checkbox; selected provider only, no automatic audio fallback.

New v29 coverage
- Original locally stored SVG icon family: 20 semantic icons per theme + 2 hero scenes.
- Home, navigation, account shortcuts, all muscle groups, page headings, cards, chat.
- Existing buttons, handlers, IDs, badges, counts and labels retained; dynamic refresh
  observes relevant content only, disconnects during edits and settles without loops.
- Theme remembered by existing browser preference; light Spicylifter/dark Powerlifter.
- All code assets use ?v=29; new SVG URLs use ?v=29; all static assets revalidate.
- Full source, static images/videos, QR and MediaPipe model included in ZIP.
