# Upper-body motion, MMA gloves and training hall

The upper body follows measured shoulder/hip heading and shoulder tilt before the punch event. Back extension can open the chest and raise the face; independently measured head orientation also allows looking up without arching or retreating. Arm directions retain their body-relative frame through the rotation. Both players receive the same bounded posture values.

The gloves and hall are original procedural geometry. The gloves have palm/knuckle/thumb padding and wrist cuffs with blue/red straps. The new hall has no placeholder spectators. Saved avatar assets, appearance and progression are preserved.

Screenshots use actual avatar assets in local Chromium with synthetic pose fixtures. They establish rendering behavior, not real-camera accuracy. Face/shoulder/hand visibility and initial upright calibration remain important; visible hips improve torso/head separation. See [controls and verification](../boxing-cage/README.md).
